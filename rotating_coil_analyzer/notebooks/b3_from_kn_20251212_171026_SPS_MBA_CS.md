## Results

### Main Field (B1)
- B1 at max current (~3100 A): ~1.19 T
- 3,501 of 3,502 turns pass ok_main threshold

### Data Quality Issues
**Critical**: The CS segment contains sporadic turns with extreme unphysical B1 values (up to ~7.4e+11 T). These outliers propagate into b3 values spanning hundreds of thousands of units. While medians at each current level are well-behaved (typically < 1 unit), means and standard deviations are dominated by these outliers.

Affected levels include 50 A, 150 A, 300 A, 350 A, 900 A, 1100 A, and several high-current levels. The root cause is likely corrupt or glitched raw data in specific turns that passes the pipeline without being flagged.

### b3 (Sextupole)
- Median b3 values across levels: -0.5 to +0.5 units (physically reasonable)
- Mean b3 (all non-zero turns): -329.7 units (skewed by outliers)
- Clean levels (e.g., 500-700 A): b3 ~ -0.5 units with std ~ 0.2 units

### Drift Correction Comparison
At clean current levels (500-700 A), legacy and weighted drift modes agree to < 0.02 units RMS for b3. The massive overall RMS difference (203,717 units) is driven entirely by outlier-contaminated levels.

## Observations

1. **AC compensation** works as expected, suppressing dipole sensitivity by ~20,000x in the compensated channel.
2. **Outlier contamination** is the dominant data quality issue. A robust outlier detection/rejection step (e.g., MAD-based sigma clipping) should be applied before computing statistics.
3. **Well-behaved levels** (500-700 A) show excellent drift-mode agreement and low turn-to-turn scatter (std ~ 0.2 units).
4. All 125 files processed without pipeline errors.

### cel/fed Safety Diagnostic
This notebook includes a `diagnose_cel_fed()` check that verifies the centre-location and feeddown corrections (cel/fed) are reliable. The diagnostic compares pipeline results with and without cel/fed, flags turns with |zR| > 1% of R_ref, and provides a SAFE/MIXED/UNSAFE recommendation. See `correction_options_reference.md` for background.

## Suggestions

1. **Add outlier rejection**: Implement a sigma-clipping or MAD-based filter to exclude turns with |B1| or |b3| beyond a reasonable threshold before computing level statistics.
2. **Investigate raw data**: The extreme outliers at specific turns likely indicate ADC glitches, digitizer overflow, or corrupt data blocks. Trace back to the raw flux waveforms to identify the root cause.
3. **Flag affected levels**: Mark levels with high std/median ratio as potentially contaminated in the summary table.
